ABOUT US

With more than three decades serving Greater Green Country,
OK, Buggy Bob's Pest Control is known for providing excellent and
reliable pest control. Our deep expertise means we're equipped to
tackle any pest issue thoroughly and efficiently. We blend our
long-standing experience with today's environmentally conscious
methods to safeguard your home and family.

SERVICES

Buggy Bob's Pest Control offers humane, closely monitored trapping for animals like armadillos, raccoons, squirrels, rats, skunks, and beavers. We use hidden traps that capture animals safely, protecting both your property and the wildlife. Our team sets up and checks these traps daily, so no animal is ever left alone.
Our priority is a trap-and-release method that avoids harm, and we aim to relocate animals to their natural habitats whenever feasible. This thorough and monitored service might take some time, but we assure you it delivers results while respecting your values. You can count on us for ethical and effective wildlife management.
